Add Volunteer Tutorial

Step 1 of 8 13%

Step 1: Fill Basic Information

Start by entering the volunteer's personal information including photo, name, contact details, gender, date of birth, and current address. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).

Step 2: Set Availability Schedule

Select the days when the volunteer is available to help. Choose their preferred department, add their skills and experience, and include their motivation for volunteering with the organization.

Step 3: Account Creation (Optional)

Optionally create a system account for the volunteer. When enabled, they will be able to access the volunteer portal with their email and a system-generated password based on their birthdate.

Step 4: Submit Volunteer Information

Once all required information is completed, click the "Add Volunteer Record" button to submit the form. The system will validate all required fields before processing.

Step 5: Review Volunteer Details

Before final submission, review all entered information in the preview modal. You can edit any details if needed or confirm to save the volunteer record to the database.

Step 6: Offline Save Feature

When there's no internet connection or an error occurs, the system automatically saves the record offline. This ensures no data is lost and will be synchronized once the connection is restored.

Step 7: Manage Offline Records

Access and manage offline saved records through the floating button. You can load saved records to edit them, manually sync individual records, or sync all records at once when connection is available.

Step 8: Success Confirmation

After successfully saving a volunteer record, you'll see a confirmation message with the volunteer ID. You can choose to add another volunteer, view all records, or close the modal. System accounts are created automatically if enabled.
