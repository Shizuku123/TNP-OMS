    Inventory Records Tutorial

    Welcome to Inventory Records

    Inventory Records helps you manage all items, supplies, and resources efficiently. Track quantities, expiry dates, categories, and monitor stock levels in real-time.

    The main dashboard shows all your inventory items organized by categories. You can search, filter, and manage items with ease. Use the category tabs to navigate between different types of items.

    Upload Existing Records

    Bulk Upload: Save time by importing multiple inventory items from an Excel file. Perfect for migrating existing records or adding large quantities of items at once.

    Steps to upload:

    1. Click "Upload Excel Records" button at the top
    2. Download the template to see the required format
    3. Fill in your inventory data in the Excel template
    4. Upload the completed file and review the mapping
    5. Confirm to add all items to your inventory

    Inventory Statistics

    Quick Overview: Monitor your inventory health with real-time statistics. Track total items, low stock alerts, near expiry items, expired products, and total value.

    Total Items: Complete count of all inventory items
    Low Stock: Items below minimum quantity threshold
    Near Expiry: Items expiring within 30 days
    Expired: Items past their expiration date

    Advanced Search & Filter

    Powerful Filtering: Find exactly what you need with advanced search options. Filter by category, status, quantity range, expiry dates, and more.

    Filter options include:

    • Search: Find items by name or description
    • Category: Filter by item category
    • Status: View available, low stock, or expired items
    • Date Range: Filter by entry or expiry dates
    • Sort: Arrange by name, quantity, or date

    Add New Item

    Quick Add: Click the "Add New Item" button to quickly add individual items to your inventory. Perfect for adding new products as they arrive.

    The + Add New Item button is located at the top right of the category section.

    Click it to open the item creation form where you can enter all details for a new inventory item.

    Item Details Form

    Complete Information: Fill in all necessary details for your inventory item. The form includes required fields and optional information for comprehensive tracking.

    Required fields:

    • Item Name: Product or item name
    • Category: Type of item (Food, Medicine, Supplies, etc.)
    • Quantity: Current stock amount
    • Unit: Measurement unit (pieces, boxes, liters, etc.)

    Optional fields:

    • Expiry Date, Supplier, Cost, Description
    • Minimum Stock Level, Storage Location
    • Photo upload for visual reference
