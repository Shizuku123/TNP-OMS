Basic Employment Requirements

These are the standard documents required for any legal employment in the Philippines:

Document Purpose Required From
Resume / Application Form Background and qualifications All applicants
Valid ID (Government-issued) Identity verification All applicants
NBI Clearance Certifies no criminal record nationwide All applicants (very important for orphanages)
Barangay Clearance Confirms good moral character and residency All applicants
Police Clearance Optional but sometimes required for added background check Recommended
Birth Certificate (PSA) Proof of age and citizenship All applicants
SSS Number / E1 Form Social Security System registration Required upon hiring
PhilHealth Number For government health insurance Required upon hiring
Pag-IBIG Number (HDMF) For housing and savings fund Required upon hiring
TIN (Tax Identification Number) For payroll and tax purposes Required upon hiring
Medical Certificate / Fit-to-Work Certifies physical fitness Required before starting work

Legal Basis:

  • • Labor Code of the Philippines – Standard employment documentation and benefits

Special Requirements for Child-Caring Institution

Since our organization cares for children, additional screening is strongly recommended or required under DSWD and child protection laws:

Document Purpose Legal / Regulatory Basis
DSWD Clearance Some positions (especially management or direct care staff) may require DSWD approval DSWD Administrative Order No. 16, series of 2012
Certificate of Good Moral Character From previous employer or school - Ensures integrity, especially when working with minors DSWD standards for child-caring staff
Psychological Evaluation / Neuro Exam Recommended for key staff (assistant director, social workers, house parents) DSWD standards for child-caring staff
Proof of Training / Certificate in Child Protection Training in child protection protocols and social work (if applicable) Child protection requirements

Legal Basis:

  • • Republic Act No. 7610 – "Special Protection of Children Against Abuse, Exploitation, and Discrimination Act"
  • • Republic Act No. 10173 – Data Privacy Act (relevant for staff handling child records)
  • • DSWD Administrative Order No. 16, s. 2012 – Standards for Residential-Based Child Caring and Placing Agencies
